# AI Behind Closed Doors: What CxOs Told Us About AI Readiness

## Overview

This repo contains the presentation and supporting material for my "AI Behind Closed Doors" talk at
[the AIBA Conference 2026 in Katowice, Poland](https://aibaconference.com/).

## Presentation

### AI Behind Closed Doors: What CxOs Told Us About AI Readiness

As [an AWS AI Competency holder](https://partners.amazonaws.com/partners/0018W0000209xghQAA/Trek2Summit),
[Trek2Summit](https://www.trek2summit.com) conducted over a dozen AI Assessments in the past six months
— working with companies big and small across aviation, finance, and FMCG.
In this session, I'll share the patterns we uncovered: what separates companies ready to scale AI from those stuck in pilot purgatory,
the fears that hold CxOs back, and the practical steps that actually move the needle.
