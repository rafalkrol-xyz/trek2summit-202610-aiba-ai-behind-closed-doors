# trek2summit-202610-aiba-ai-behind-closed-doors

## Overview

This repo contains the presentation and supporting material for my "AI Behind Closed Doors" talk at AIBA Conference 2026 in Katowice, Poland.

## Presentation

### AI Behind Closed Doors: What CxOs Told Us About AI Readiness

As an AWS AI Competency holder, Trek2Summit conducted over a dozen AI Assessments in the past six months — working with companies big and small across aviation, finance, and FMCG. In this session, I'll share the patterns we uncovered: what separates companies ready to scale AI from those stuck in pilot purgatory, the fears that hold CxOs back, and the practical steps that actually move the needle.
